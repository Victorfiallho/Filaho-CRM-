Questions

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The visit and the quote are free, with no commitment.

I already bought my cabinets. Can you install them?

Yes. We install cabinets you already have: square, level and secure, with fillers, trim and hardware.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the job. A cabinet swap can be done in a day, while a full remodel takes a few weeks. You get the timeline with your quote, before anything starts.

Should I send photos?

Yes, please. Photos, measurements or inspiration images help us understand your project when we connect.
